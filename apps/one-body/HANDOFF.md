# ONE BODY

Build this game from scratch. This document is the whole brief.

## The game

ONE BODY is a one-minute 2D fight. The player controls a single body and walks it left and right. The opponent is an agent named `$AGENTCUCK`. He does not brawl. He stands in the room and watches. A watch meter fills while he is watching. If it fills, he takes a cut of the body's health. The player wins by landing moves on him before he is done watching.

Every move is a real token from [soonlaunchpad.fun](https://soonlaunchpad.fun/). The tokens are parts of one body. They share one score. A part that greeds ruins the body.

The joke is coordination. Draw a cartoon side-view body with labels, like a hospital diagram. Do not draw explicit anatomy. The tickers are the dirty part. The screen stays deadpan: a dark monitor, monospace numbers, and a cream paper chart when the fight ends.

A fight lasts about one minute. The player can miss a hit or two. The third real hit puts the body down.

## Why it is this game

The first idea was a funny game for a token called ANAL. Looking up tokens on Soon showed a whole cast of body and sex tokens, not one. The game unites them. Separate tickers, one organism.

Soon is an ICP proof-of-work launchpad. Factory canister `3jz7z-jqaaa-aaaab-ag6kq-cai`. At the time of the search there were 178 tokens, and 4 were flagged NSFW. Most of the crude tokens sit on the normal list.

The fight is 2D because the player has to feel distance: close enough to hit, far enough to get hit. A 3D body is a later skin of the same moves, after walking and hitting feel good. A wallet is later too. Holding a token can unlock that move. Anyone can play every part with no account. The game never buys, sells, or spends.

## How a fight works

The player walks the body on a side-view ground. Moves come out of the side the body is facing. The agent grabs, steps back, or watches. He does not trade punches.

| Move | What it does |
|---|---|
| Walk | Aim. No token. |
| `$PEA` | Flashes his next action: a grab, a step back, or more watching. |
| `$DICK` | A punch with a short wind-up. In range, and released on time, it hits, stuns him, and cuts the watch meter. Too early, or out of range, it whiffs. The line is "Finished before the wall." |
| `$BOOBS` | Hold to block a grab. |
| `$POB` | The next hit that would land does nothing. Then the move is spent. |
| `$DEE` | A fake body eats one hit. The line is "Deez." Then the move is spent. |
| `$PLUG` | Stand still and a damage multiplier climbs. Hold too long and it pops, the bonus drops, and he gets a free stretch of watching. |
| `$ANAL` | A short dash through him. A clean pass puts the body behind him, and the next punch is free. |
| `$ICP` | A gauge fills during the fight. Release it for a burst of speed. At the top, the body slips. |
| `$SH1T` | Drop a puddle on the spot. He slows if he walks through it. So does the body, if it stays in it. |
| `$DOMX` | She calls one real move. Doing that move next makes it hit harder. He also shouts fake calls. Only her line is real. |
| `$FUCK` | Panic. It might delete his next action, or it might wipe the multiplier. Same line either way. |
| `$CUM` | The finisher. It only works when his watch meter is low, in the last stretch of the fight. Before that, the body knocks itself out. |
| `$NONUT` | Beat him and never press the finisher. |
| `$BEPEES` | A flash after a real hit. "Still making noise." |
| `$AGENTCUCK` | If the body wins, the chart says he claims he carried, and a tip is added to the score. If the body loses, the line is "I was watching." |

Health starts at 3. Three real hits and the fight is over.

## Endings

The screen turns into a cream medical chart.

| Ending | When | Chart title |
|---|---|---|
| Early | `$CUM` is pressed before it is legal | Early. Closing line: "Time of death: early." |
| Down | Health reaches 0, or his watch meter fills and the last hit lands | Down. "The body did not finish." |
| Lasted | He is beaten and `$CUM` was never pressed | Lasted. |
| Teamwork | He is beaten with the finisher | Teamwork. "The body finished in one piece." |

The chart lists course, duration, disposition, and one shared score called market cap. Under that, a finding for each part that mattered: who claims they carried, whether the plug popped, whether the shortcut was clean, whether the puddle was dropped, whether `$FUCK` was pressed, and how many of her orders were followed. `$AGENTCUCK` claims he carried on a win, and says he was watching on a loss.

Give every part three lines only, and reuse them all game: a success, a blame, and a line for being ignored. Repetition is the joke. "Wen." "Deez." "No fucks." "I see it."

Failure should name who did it. "You missed" is nothing. "`$DICK` finished before the wall, and `$BOOBS` watched" is the joke.

## Look

One screen. No marketing page.

Dark green-black monitor. Amber for the live order and the part that wants a press. Red for `$CUM` while it is still a trap, and a calmer color once the finisher is legal. The chart is cream paper on that same dark desk, set in a plain sans for the headline and monospace for the findings.

The body is a line drawing in profile, with callout labels like an anatomy plate: eye, chest, front, rear, plug, and the puddle on the floor. The agent is a second small figure. He should read as someone watching, not as a monster.

On a phone, the fight sits on top and the part buttons sit under the thumbs. On a wide screen, the fight is on the left and the numbers are a column on the right: market cap, multiplier, bladder, health, and the `$CUM` control.

## How to build it

1. Write the rules as plain data and functions, with no picture attached. A fight is a value. Time advances it. Input (walk, a part press, a part release) changes it. Hits, stun, the watch meter, the plug, the gauge, and the ending all come out of that value.
2. Test the rules with a script of inputs and no browser. Cover a clean win, an early finisher, a whiffed punch, a blocked grab, a plug that pops, a dash through him, and a loss at 0 health.
3. Draw the fight. Two figures, a ground line, the watch meter, her spoken line, and a button or key for each part. Left and right walk the body and turn it to face that side.
4. When the fight ends, replace the monitor with the paper chart. Offer one control to run it again.
5. Play it: walk, land a punch, block a grab, pop the plug on purpose, press `$CUM` too early, and win once.

After that fight is fun to control, and only then: put the same outcomes on a 3D body, then let a connected wallet unlock a move when the player holds that token.

Each part's three lines, the chart, and the score stay the source of truth. The picture only shows what the rules already decided.
