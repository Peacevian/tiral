# Kyadondo: the first hangout

The first of the places players go to and meet (clubs, hangouts, markets).
Kyadondo Rugby Grounds on Jinja Road, Lugogo: music, pork and drinks, open
any time, busiest in the evenings and on Saturdays (rugby day). Decided
18 Oct 2026 (defaults).

## The real place (what the venue copies)
- Jinja Road, Nakawa/Lugogo, on the corner of the Lugogo By-Pass, about
  3.5 km east of the city centre (0.3252°N, 32.6076°E). Across Jinja Road:
  Lugogo Mall; next door: KCC Grounds and the Lugogo tennis courts.
- About six acres of green belt (OSM: ~160 × 130 m): the main pitch
  (~100 × 55 m, long side along Jinja Road), a training field, the clubhouse
  on the north (Jinja Road) side, and a row of small buildings along the south
  edge (bars, kitchens, changing rooms).
- Home of Heathens RFC; built 1999–2000 by players who crossed the road from
  Kampala RFC (now Legends). Rugby on Saturdays, concerts, screenings,
  corporate events. Known for loud music and pork after matches.
- The July 2010 bombing happened here. Nothing in the game refers to it.
- Sources: Wikipedia (Kyadondo Rugby Club, Lugogo), visitkampala.kcca.go.ug,
  Monitor, The Independent, OpenStreetMap (way 930325557, pitch 67599793).

## The venue in the game
Its own small walkable scene (like Home), reached by a ride from the map.
Layout (pitch along x, Jinja Road to the north, 1 unit = 1 m):
- Grass grounds with trees round the edge; the pitch with lines, H posts and
  four floodlight masts.
- North side: the clubhouse (two floors, the club sign) and a covered stand.
- South side: the pork joint (grill and smoke), the bar (counter, fridges),
  the stage with a DJ booth and speakers, tents with plastic chairs and
  tables, toilets.
- The gate on Jinja Road, boda parking outside it, Lugogo Mall across the road.

## Economy (real Kampala prices)
| | Price |
|---|---|
| Entry | free by day; 10,000 on event nights |
| Pork | quarter kilo 15,000 · half 30,000 · kilo platter (share) 55,000 |
| Drinks | soda 3,000 · beer 7,000 · bucket of beers 35,000 · spirits from 60,000 |
| A round for your table | the drink × people at the table |
| Boda there | 8,000–10,000 |

What you get: stress goes down (music, friends), energy from food, and
followers/clout for buying rounds and dancing. Later: work a shift at the
grill or the bar (like the rolex stand), and own the pork joint (a business).

## Busy times
Open any time. The simulated crowd and the music follow Kampala time:
quiet mornings, busier from 5pm, full in the evening, and Saturday is rugby
day (a simulated match in the afternoon, the party after).
